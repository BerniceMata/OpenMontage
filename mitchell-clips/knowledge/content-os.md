# BERNICE MATA CONTENT OS — v1
### One filming day. Seven posts. One scoreboard. Built from 8 extracted creator systems, your niche map, your 334K words of transcripts, and two ad-library studies.

**The thesis this machine runs on (yours, validated by Leila Hormozi):** content is getting people interested in what you're interested in to watch you. Distribution is the moat. Content is leverage at scale — so you can get paid to accomplish what you want.

**The guardrail (non-negotiable):** this system is budgeted at **~6 hours/week** and never touches dental calling hours. Content is the long game; dental is the 90-day game. The moment content prep eats a call block, the system is being used as avoidance and gets called out.

## 1. REQUIREMENTS

**Functional:** turn one weekly filming session into 1 YouTube long-form + 5–7 Instagram posts, test hooks systematically, detect outliers against your own median, double down on winners.

**Non-functional:** sustainable solo at ~6 hrs/week · $0 budget (phone, CapCut, IG native) · survives a bad Hostra week (degraded mode below) · every component has a decision rule so nothing requires fresh thinking on a tired day.

**Constraints:** one operator + Claude. Claude researches, sorts, drafts, tracks, and flags. **Mitchell films, speaks, and decides.** AI never writes the final words — 3 of 8 extracted operators state this as a hard rule, and your channel doc already agrees.

## 3. THE SIX COMPONENTS

### 3.1 Idea Bank — already seeded, never empty
Lives in the project. Contents right now: **110 quotable lines** from your transcripts, **35 cut-ready clip passages** with line numbers, **30 confession moments**, the **5 validated topics** from the niche map, and the **100-hook bank** (separate doc). Entry rule: a new idea only graduates to filming if it maps to a validated topic + hook structure + format (the 3-3-3 slate — already validated, no re-checking). Everything else goes to the bank and waits. This kills the orange-tree switch: new idea ≠ new video, new idea = new row.

### 3.2 Film Day — one session, everything captured
**Base spec (sustainable): Sunday, 2.5 hrs.**
- **Block 1 (60–75 min):** YouTube long-form — you talking through 2–3 ideas from the bank, the way you already do. This is the ore.
- **Block 2 (45 min):** 2–3 native reels, shot in the validated formats: **CAR** (parked, night, face lit by dash, one take) and **BED** (static talking head, 70–160s, one take). Script = a hook from the bank + your actual thought. No full scripts — hook line written, spoken from there. One take, two max.
- **Block 3 (20 min):** b-roll for the Scene Library (Jun Yuh) — you walking, journaling, driving, at church, tying braids, working. Labeled folders. Batch once, reuse forever. This unlocks the **VO format** (voiceover over footage of you alone — highest outlier ratios in your niche map) without ever filming again.

**10× spec (what "do more" actually means here):** the Hormozi/Cardone lever at zero followers is not three filming days — it's **never missing a week for 52 weeks.** Every one of the 8 extracted operators is 900–5,000 posts deep; the 10× is the library. THAT SAID: when the base spec runs 4 consecutive weeks without a miss, add a second film day (Wednesday, 60 min, reels only) and push to 10–12 posts/week. Volume is earned by consistency, not declared by ambition. TMYP TMYG — after the machine holds.

**Degraded mode (bad Hostra week):** skip filming entirely. The Clip Factory runs on existing YouTube footage — you have 35 cut-ready passages banked. A zero-filming week still ships 5 posts. **There is no legal reason to miss a week.**

### 3.3 Clip Factory — where the 6 hrs/week mostly goes
Caleb's sort on every long-form: **Clip or Concept?**
- **Clip** = the passage stands alone (hook, turn, landing line already in the take) → cut it, caption it, ship it.
- **Concept** = the idea is strong but the delivery is buried → re-shoot it native as a CAR/BED reel next film day, or run it as VO over Scene Library footage.

**Edit spec (Mino's Minimum Viable Editing — total per clip: ≤20 min):**
- Captions: 2–3 words per line, classic font, 20% shadow, nonchalant placement
- Cut ALL dead space — even 0.2s
- Background music: instrumental, ~20% volume, matched to intent (educational/emotional/storytelling/positive), non-copyright. Music ≠ trending audio.
- **End at the peak (Yasin):** find the emotional high point, cut there, delete everything after. Recency bias means the ending is the video.
- Length target: **45–95s** (your niche's validated winning zone — sub-30s loses here)
- Keep: pauses, laughs, self-corrections, "bro," the malaprops. That's the texture. Cut: schedule recaps, triple disclaimers, shout-out chains (your rambling mode, per the voice profile).

### 3.4 Packaging Gate — every post passes or doesn't ship
1. **Hook** from the 100-bank (or a new mint logged to the bank). Stack three: something to SEE (your face/setting doing something), something to READ (cover text ≤4 words), something to HEAR (the hook line spoken first — no wind-up).
2. **Broad hook, narrow value** — the first line is wide enough for a stranger; the specificity comes inside. Never niche the hook.
3. **Caption = the one sentence the viewer carries.** Lowercase, first person, standalone. It's the takeaway, not a description. (Validated on 5+ accounts; it's what every 100× outlier in your niche shares.)
4. **Headline discipline (ads studies, both libraries independently):** 4–6 words, question or command, no adjectives, no superlatives.
5. **No hashtags.** (Dead in your lane — zero qualifying creators surfaced from ~30 tags; both mega-accounts use none. Brock: hashtags don't grow accounts.)
6. **The one-idea test:** "This video is about ___, but it's really saying ___." Can't fill it in → back to the bank.
7. **First frame confirms the click** (Mino) — cover text and first spoken line must match.

### 3.5 Publish Queue — CTA doctrine
**Cadence:** 5–7/week, 1/day max in Phase 1. Meta's native scheduler is fine (Brock tested it: no difference) — or Composio posts it.
**CTA rules (resolving the Brock-vs-Caleb conflict with Caleb's Give/Ask + your niche data):**
- **5 of 7 posts: ZERO ask.** The biggest outliers in your niche have no CTA — comments come from the idea being arguable.
- **1 of 7: a real question** — specific, from the video ("which conversation have you been avoiding for months?"). Never "comment below."
- **1 of 7: follow-framing** — "follow for more perspective" / a series breadcrumb.
- **No comment-keyword funnels yet.** Nothing to sell = nothing to gate. The funnel layer bolts on later without redesign (every operator's funnel starts the same way: keyword → DM → asset).
**Cross-posting:** same clips to TikTok + Shorts (Lake Method-lite: re-export native, don't waterfall watermarks). IG is the Eye of Sauron platform; the others are maintenance.

### 3.6 Scoreboard — what Friday reads
Metrics (chosen by convergence, NOT raw views):
| Metric | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Posts shipped this week** | 5+ | The only metric you fully control. This is the calls-counter of content. |
| **Outlier multiple** (views ÷ your rolling 30-post median) | ≥3× flags a winner | Caleb's baseline method; your median IS the honest denominator at any size |
| **View-to-follower conversion** | ≥1% on any post ≥10K views | Sam Gaudet's gate; the anti-vanity metric |
| **Comments per view** + what they say | watch for "I felt seen" language | Your channel promise, measured |
| **Hook ID per post** | logged always | So winners are attributable to a hook, not a vibe |

**Decision rules (no thinking required on Friday):**
- Post hits ≥3× median → **clone it** (Ava/Caleb): same hook different topic, same topic different format (CAR→VO), part 2. Minimum 3 clones per winner. Repeat the hook until it stops working.
- Hook used 3× with all results <1× median → retire the hook, log it dead.
- 2 consecutive flat weeks (no post >1.5× median) → change HOOKS, not topics. 4 flat weeks → change formats. Topics only change if comments say the idea missed — the topics are validated.
- Someone's comment contains a better sentence than your caption → it's next week's video (audience-language mining, every operator does this).

## 4. PHASES (Caleb's Accordion, resolving the cadence conflict)

**Phase 1 — EXPAND (weeks 1–8):** 5–7/week, standalone swings, maximum hook variety from the bank, no asks. Goal is DATA: find your 2–3 outlier hooks. You chose the ceiling model (100 hooks, post, see what works) — correct at zero followers, and it's Smoak's path not Jack's. The numbered series (floor model) is deliberately deferred: series buy a floor you don't need yet and cost a ceiling you do.
**Phase 2 — CONTRACT (weeks 9–16):** cut hook variety to the 3 proven winners, raise reps on them, launch ONE numbered series as the floor-builder (candidate: "Seeking Perspective, Day N" — it's already your channel's stated mission, verbatim: "me seeking perspective is how I grow").
**Phase 3 — CONVERT (when: 10K followers OR 3 posts >250K):** comment-keyword funnel, lead asset, email. Ads studies un-park here. Not before. Don't build the machine before there's a thing at the end of it.

## 5. TRADE-OFFS MADE (explicit, revisitable)

- **Ceiling over floor** (standalone swings, no series in Phase 1) — revisit at week 8 with data.
- **IG-first, YouTube-as-source** — YouTube stays your thinking room; IG is distribution. Revisit if YouTube subs outpace IG followers 3:1.
- **Education-flavored storytelling over pure entertainment** (Ava's 68× follower conversion beats Brock's share-maximizing) — your lane already votes this way.
- **6 hrs/week cap** — deliberately under-ambitious vs your 10× instinct, because the failure mode being engineered against is not "too few posts," it's "week 3 collapse." The 10× unlock is written into 3.2 with its trigger condition.
- **No paid layer** — Kajabi/Skool lessons are embedded in the Packaging Gate (headline rules, failed-alternative hook, current-state opens); everything else parks until Phase 3.
