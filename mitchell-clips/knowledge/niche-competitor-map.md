## PART 2 — The eight validated patterns

Cross-verified: each of these won on 3+ different accounts, and the ones marked ✅✅ showed up independently in *both* sweeps.

**1. ✅✅ Long beats short. 45–180 seconds is the winning length.**
by.jdawg 36–53s · jake.hrtllsa 29–94s · henrybpan 61–102s · dierce_ 53–81s · jellognv 29–90s · john.ridgeway 72–156s · honestyouthpastor 108/135/124s · jpokluda 100/137/178s. The "keep it under 30 seconds" advice is **wrong for this lane.** Matches Ava's own finding that :59 beat :14 by 68× on follower conversion.

**2. ✅✅ The caption IS the video's one sentence — lowercase, first person, standalone.**
*"I still believe in people. I just don't trust as easily as I used to."* · *"everyone is their own main character."* · *"i hope you don't think i'm selfish . . ."* · *"i haven't been honest with myself at all recently"* · *"Be honest with others, but also yourself. I learned I had to do that today."* Five+ accounts. The caption is not a description — it's the takeaway written so it works alone on the Explore page. This is exactly your channel doc's *"one sentence the viewer can carry"* rule, and it's what every 100×+ outlier shares.

**3. ✅✅ The hook indicts the speaker, never the viewer.**
Not one top outlier in this entire dataset opens with *"you need to."* Every single account's biggest reel opens by admitting the creator's own fault: *"I'm not angry that you didn't choose me"* · *"i haven't been honest with myself"* · *"i hope you don't think i'm selfish"* · *"I really didn't want to make this video"* · *"I learned I had to do that today."*

**4. ✅✅ A real question is the CTA — and on the biggest reels there's no CTA at all.**
jake.hrtllsa, chuafilms, ethnchristiansen, john.ridgeway, dierce_ — **zero asks** on their top performers. Comments come from the idea being arguable, not from being asked (john.ridgeway 6.8K and 7.9K comments, ethnchristiansen 8.7K). Where a question exists it's specific: *"where do we actually draw the line in one-sided friendships?"* · *"On a scale of 1-10, how confident are you…"*

**5. ✅ Faith through family, legacy, and lived life — not doctrine.**
Bethke's 49.5× outlier is fathers-and-children; his doctrine posts sit at median. jpokluda's outliers are marriage and heaven-as-personal-question. Jack's top reels are family and *"what success is really about."* by.jdawg: *"Maybe the love you are yearning for is actually just Jesus."* Strongest emotional multiplier found in the whole dataset.

**6. ✅ Contrarian reframe of something the audience already reveres.**
*"The disciples were absolute cowards"* (5.7×) · *"It's not a perfect childhood, it's a father who loves Jesus"* (49.5×) · *"consistency isn't about crushing every workout"* · *"Too much discipline is poison."* This is your doc's *"I used to think X, but now I think Y"* formula — validated four times over.

**7. ✅ The numbered daily series buys a floor, not a ceiling.**
by.jdawg "9/365" · jellognv "303/365" · rojeru_ "96/365" · henrybpan "day 8 no 🚬" · jacksdayinthelife "Day 145 of How to Not Be a Psychopath." Jack's series produced a **261K median with zero outliers.** Smoak has a 214K median with one 10M spike. Same tier, opposite strategies — **decide which you're optimizing before you start one.**

**8. ✅ Concrete beats abstract in the opening. Every time.**
A named person, a named number, a named question — never a concept. Smoak (*"the high performer question"*) · honestyouthpastor (*"Kenneth Copeland"*, *"Montanus"*) · jpokluda (*"On a scale of 1-10"*) · Nathaniel Drew (named guest + credential). **Zero outliers opened on an abstract noun.**

## PART 4 — What this changes

**Kill:** hashtags, sub-30-second cuts, any video that opens with "you," any video that opens on an abstract noun, and the instinct to model @higherupwellness. He's a 990K account monetizing seminars with a flat curve. You're at zero. Model @ronanbuilds — **18 posts, 8.8K followers, 2.5M views, 284×.**

**Keep:** everything in your channel doc about caption-as-takeaway, the real-question CTA, and *"I used to think X, now I think Y."* The data validated all three independently.

**Decide once:** Jack's model (numbered daily series → high floor, no ceiling) or Smoak's (standalone swings → low floor, 10M ceiling). Both work at the same size. You cannot run both, and picking neither is how you end up at median forever.

**The uncomfortable one:** the highest-multiple video in this entire dataset is a 7.4K-follower kid, in a dark car, no hook graphic, no editing, saying one true thing for 34 seconds — 3.9 million views. There is no production problem standing between you and this. There's only the filming.
