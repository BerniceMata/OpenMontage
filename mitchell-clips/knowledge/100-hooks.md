**Topics:** WORDS (words/communication) · SEEN (being seen/performing) · PERSP (perspective) · BECOME (discipline/execution/becoming) · FAITH (lived faith) · HUMAN (human behavior)
**Formats:** CAR (night car confession) · BED (bedroom talking head) · VO (voiceover over b-roll of you alone) · CLIP (cut from existing YouTube footage — line numbers in the clip candidates list)

**Usage rules (from the research, non-negotiable):**
1. Speak the hook as the FIRST words out of your mouth. No "hey guys," no context.
2. Cover text = ≤4 words from the hook. First frame confirms the click.
3. When a hook produces a ≥3× outlier: repeat it until it stops working. Same hook, new topic. Same hook, new format. That's the whole game.
4. Log every use with the hook number so the scoreboard can attribute wins.
