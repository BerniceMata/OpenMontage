### Everything @personalbrandlaunch teaches about posting, content strategy, and growth

**Who:** Ava Yuergens, 21. Founder of Personal Brand Launch LLC. IG 1M, TikTok ~2.4M, YouTube ~820K. Built the business IG 0 → 500K in two years. Agency does done-for-you short form: 15–30 reels/month.

## 0. The one-paragraph version

Mine other people's outlier videos in your niche using a hard ratio (5× views-to-followers). Steal the **hook and the format only — never the value.** Transplant your own expertise into that proven container. Keep the hook broad so it maximizes reach, deliver narrow value, and put the filtering in the CTA. When a video hits, clone it across formats and swap the nouns until it stops working.

> 🎙 *"I would go to the Instagram Explorer page, type in my niche. I would say like 100 videos over a million views. Take the hooks from those videos, and I would write 30 scripts with those viral hooks, but my own niche expertise."*

## 1. Research

### The 5× Rule ⭐🎙
A video is an **outlier** only if it got at least **5× the account's follower count in views**.

### The Rule of 3-3-3 ⭐
Before making anything, find 3+ videos on the same TOPIC, 3+ using the same HOOK structure, 3+ using the same FORMAT, all passing 5×. If you can't find three, don't film it.

### Research operations ◇
20 minutes a day of intentional scrolling. Sort Feed extension, ViewStats, Hemingway Editor (5th-grade reading level). Agency spends 10–12 hours/month researching top short-form per client niche.

### Copy hooks and formats, never value ◇

## 2. Awareness: the 4 Levels ⭐

| Level | Name | Reach |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Product-Aware | Lowest (1K–5K) |
| 2 | Solution-Aware | Low (40K–80K) |
| 3 | **Problem-Aware** | High (700K–1M) — the working target |
| 4 | Unaware | Highest (1M–4M) but converts worst |

## 3. Hooks

- **Priority: Hook → Script → Format → Editing.**
- **Stack three hooks in the first three seconds:** something to see, read, hear.
- **Broad hook, narrow value, niche CTA.** Don't niche your hook like an ad.
- **Find one hook and beat it to death.** Repeat it until it stops working.
- **1 Idea → 7 Hooks.** Write to a fifth-grade reading level.

## 4. Script structure

- **HOOK → VALUE → CTA**, designed against the retention dip.
- Agency script anatomy: Notes (format, how to film) · Bulleted script · Caption · Subtitles.
- **"Explain X in 1 line."** Every step capped at 1–2 lines.
- Structures: Hero's Journey (6 steps) · The Challenge (9) · The Big Goal (9) · The Case Study (6).
- **Telling a story ≠ storytelling:** telling = 100 views; storytelling (show what happened, structure, visuals, written before filming) = 3M.
- **Same views, 68× the followers:** 1M views → 154 followers vs 10.5K. Difference: researched outlier hooks, educational storytelling over entertainment, :59 over :14, scripted Hook → Value → CTA. **Follower conversion per view is the goal, not views.**

## 5. Format: the 15 containers ⭐
Tutorials · Testimonials · Tips/Hacks · Right vs Wrong · Comparisons · Before & After · Storytelling · Voiceover · Shot/Angle Changes · Visual · Clones · Talking Back & Forth · Green Screen · Whiteboard · Reaction.
Format cloning: when a script hits, clone across formats and swap the nouns. **Month 4 was the breakout.**

## 6. Trust and authority at zero ⭐
1. Effort ("I read 337 books") · 2. Failures · 3. Public self-experiment series · 4. Authority hacking · 5. Real social proof once you have it. What works: vulnerable, admits mistakes, shows real life.

## 7. Music ⭐
Background music ≠ trending audio. Match music to intent (educational, emotional, storytelling, positive). Non-copyright.

## 8. Posting and ops
- 15–30 reels/month (4–7/week), repurposed across TikTok and Shorts.
- ≤60s, 9:16, but :59 beats :14 for conversion.
- Instagram over TikTok ◇. Post from US IPs. Native scheduler performs on par; retest quarterly. Don't buy followers. **Don't let AI write the final script.**

## 9. The conversion machine
Comment keyword → ManyChat DM → proof-heavy landing page → VSL → sales call. Follower-gated freebie. One reel: 7.9M views → ~13,000 comment leads → 100–200 clients. Her free 1,000-hooks asset took ~300 hours.

## 10. Don't-do list
Don't niche your hook like an ad · don't copy value · don't buy followers · don't let AI write the final script · no copyrighted music · nothing unvalidated 3-3-3 · don't chase views, chase follower conversion.

## 11. Not on record about
Hashtags, retention thresholds, caption formatting. Closest: ◇ "systematize the first 3 seconds, measure the next 30."

# Translation to the channel

**Transfers directly:**
1. **3-3-3 replaces "I had a thought."** The enforcement mechanism for "do not make videos just because the creator has a thought."
2. **4 Levels explains the ceiling.** "You're Not Confused, You're Just Not Moving" is Level 3. "The Camera Effect" is Level 4. Anything about the channel itself is Level 1 and will die.
3. **Broad hook, narrow value, niche CTA** is the highest-leverage change. Widen the first 5 seconds, keep the value specific, let the CTA filter.
4. **One hook, repeated until it dies.** The cocoa rule applied to packaging.

**Doesn't transfer:** the comment-keyword funnel. Nothing to sell on that channel yet.

**Tension worth naming:** "steal the hook, transplant your expertise" conflicts with raw honesty *if* it drives the idea. Use it for the container only: her hook, her format, your actual thought. The moment a topic is picked because it validated rather than because you mean it, the channel's only real asset is traded for reach.
