# Creator Systems Vol. 2
### The seven new operators, extracted the same way as Ava — reels frame-by-frame + long-form sources

Method: Instagram's in-page media API for real play counts and captions, scrubber-spritesheet frame reading for burned-in teaching, plus podcasts/courses/interviews off-platform. Everything quoted is theirs; inference is flagged.

Mitchell's observation checks out: **all of these accounts are 900–5,000+ posts deep.** Ava 1,349 · Brock 5,099 · Jun 1,453 · Leila 3,517 · viralvideo.club 1,192 · Caleb 896 · Sam 312 (but daily in August). The library IS the moat. Nobody on this list got paid off their first 100 posts.

## 1. Yasin Mammeri — @viralvideo.club (639K, 1,192 posts)

**Who:** "Turning founders into the Oracle of their industry." 0 → 1M+ cross-platform in 25 months, £1.3M business, consults for Red Bull and Instagram.

**His system in one paragraph:** hooks are the only leverage left now that follower counts stopped driving views; package with psychology (Bluff Hook: controversial statement + refute it), **end every video at its emotional peak** — recency bias means viewers judge the whole video by its ending (his own case study: same footage re-cut, 612K → 12.5M) — diagnose with retention-graph shapes (early cliff = bad hook, late drop = bad ending, steady decline = bad video), and build the business behind the views.

**Most transferable rules:**
- **"END IT HERE"** — cut at the peak, delete everything after.
- **Retention Graphs 101** — fix only the part the graph indicts.
- **7 idea prompts:** what you hate about your industry · where it's going · what people misunderstand · scar tissue from mistakes · the one concept that saves your audience years · your expertise in 60s · a funny story.
- Got banned at 600K followers — **build the email list**; never all-in on rented land.
- 3 paths: **deliver insight (hard, AI-proof) · signal status · create resonance.**
- Long-form (11–12 min) is the current trust edge; IG is becoming TV — **build episodic series now.**
- "Content is like poker. You might lose the hand even if you played perfectly."

## 2. Brock Johnson — @brock11johnson (945K, 5,099 posts)

**System:** pure volume + funnel industrialization. 2–4 posts/day, three content tiers (snackable memes for shares, tactical lists for saves, a daily public-goal series for community), ManyChat comment keyword on virtually every post.

**Rules:**
- **"80% of my time, energy, and effort goes on the first 20% of the video."** 200-view jail is a hook problem.
- **Shares are the algorithm's #1 metric.** Five share-worthy types: motivational, call-to-arms, relatable, controversial, news.
- **SHARE:** Simple, Hook, Authentic, Relatable, Entertainment.
- **7 retention tricks:** Zeigarnik effect, open loops, callout targeting, 2-second visual-change rule, specific social proof, negativity bias, loss aversion.
- **Create daily ≠ post daily.** Films 2×/month, ~16 reels per session, ~37 min/day on IG.
- Hashtags don't grow accounts. Levers: SEO, share-worthy content, upcycling, hyper-frequency, carousels, variety, Trial Reels.
- Scheduling vs manual: **no difference.**
- Uses **Threads as a free content tester.**

## 3. Leila Hormozi — @leilahormozi (1.5M, 3,517 posts)

**System:** ~8 reels/week, ~85% clipped from long-form, IG as trailer distribution. **Post for people who will pay you, not people who will watch you.**

**Rules:**
- Distribution-as-moat: *"The one thing you have left is your identity as a human… if you own that distribution, you can build whatever product or service you want."* This is Mitchell's "content is leverage at scale" thesis.
- **"Ham and Garlic"** — wrap the important message in an engaging hook.
- **"Double Dip"** — repurpose what you already wrote.
- AI is an **editor, not a writer**. Never delegates idea generation or voice.
- Build shows around **questions your audience already asks you.**
- Scale reach-per-post via packaging before scaling frequency.

## 4. Caleb Ralston — @calebralston (186K, 896 posts) ⭐ densest system

**Who:** Grew GaryVee's TikTok 300K→3.5M; Hormozi content employee #1, 1.2M→11.5M.

**System:** reverse-engineer the brand from the outcome, expand to absurd volume to gather data, contract onto proven winners, never optimize for views — viral is "the fastest way to build an audience that will never buy from you."

**Frameworks:**
- **Brand Journey:** What outcome? What must you be known for? What must you do? What must you learn?
- **Accordion Method:** expand to extreme volume for ~2 months → contract, raise effort on what won. *"Quality is subjective. The only people who know what quality is is your audience."*
- **3-Question Content Test:** understandable? graspable quickly? valid in 3+ scenarios?
- **Clip or Concept:** mine long-form for moments — clips post as-is; concepts get rebuilt natively. **This is the exact rule for Mitchell's YouTube-clips plan.**
- **Squeeze the orange:** when a concept outperforms, re-run it across every format.
- **Named concepts** must be easy to Remember, Repeat, Reference.
- **Student vs Expert:** without W's, be the guinea pig documenting the journey. Share L's to make the W's believable.
- **Origin-story element in every piece.**
- **Pond > ocean.** 80/20 expertise/personality. **Give/Ask:** give in organic, ask in paid. **Eye of Sauron:** dominate one platform.
- Repetition doctrine: say the same thing over and over in slightly different ways.
- Report **multipliers vs your own baseline** (150K on a 100K baseline = 1.5×).
- **3-year no-expectations horizon.** Preproduction = 80% of the formula.

## 5. Jun Yuh — @jun_yuh (5.3M, 1,453 posts)

**System:** storytelling IS the niche. Viral emotional story reels prove the promise; teaching reels in one rigid container convert.

**Rules:**
- **3P story arc: Problem → Pursuit → Payoff.** Never open with success — "our human common denominator is struggle." **Best scripting frame for this channel in the whole dataset.**
- **"Tell your story and become the niche."** The parts you hide are the parts people connect to most.
- **3-1-2-1 weekly:** Attract ×3 · Nurture ×1 · Position ×2 · Convert ×1.
- **Scene Library:** 50+ labeled b-roll folders. Batch once, film never.
- **"Complexity kills consistency."** · **"Don't go viral. Go valuable. Then you'll stay."**
- AI rule: **AI never writes your stories — it interviews you.**

## 6. Mino Lee — @minolee.mp4 (534K, 224 posts)

**Rules:**
- **Viewer-first:** define ONE specific viewer before editing anything.
- **Research-first virality:** take the exact sentence structures, camera angles, and songs from viral videos and apply them to your niche.
- **Minimum Viable Editing:** captions 3 words max per line · 20% shadow · classic font · **cut even 0.2s of dead space** · instrumental audio at 20% volume.
- **View-jail tiers:** different fixes at 100 / 1K / 10K / 100K views.
- Three-act storytelling wave + "click confirmation" — the first frame must confirm what the click promised.

## 7. Sam Gaudet — @sam.gaudet (63K, 312 posts)

**Who:** Creative Director for Dan Martell since 16. Scaled Martell 10K → 8.8M.

**Frameworks:**
- **Content Pyramid (top = everything):** TOPIC > Thumbnail > Title > Hook > Content > Story > Analogy > Example. Topic selection beats all execution below it.
- **H.E.I.T.:** Hook (Context, Contrarian, Create intrigue) → Explain → Illustrate → Takeaway.
- **Five Ms:** Model → Map → Make → Multiply → Measure.
- **70/20/10:** 70% proven formats · 20% variation · 10% innovation.
- **C.C.N.:** every topic serves Core, Casual, or New.
- **Lake Method:** platform-native distribution per platform, not waterfall reposting.
- **R.I.A. — Results In Advance.**
- **View-to-follower target: 1%.**
- **Promise-payoff gap:** pay off the click immediately or they swipe.
- Script word-swaps: and→but/so · I→you · tips→secrets · don't→never.
- Topics > editing quality · 2-minute shoots per short · frameworks in ≤2-page artifacts.

# What's already answered for the system phase
The pipeline shape: YouTube long-form → Clip-or-Concept sort → clips post as-is, concepts rebuilt native → 3-1-2-1 slots → H.E.I.T./3P scripts → Minimum Viable Editing → end-at-the-peak → batch cadence.
Scoreboard metrics by convergence: view-to-follower % (1%), 3s hook rate, watch-through, shares, saves, comments-per-view, outlier multiple vs own median. NOT raw views.
Composio → Instagram gives the scoreboard live data and handles publishing.
